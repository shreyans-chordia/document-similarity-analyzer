Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services such as servers, storage, databases, networking and software over the internet. Rather than buying and maintaining physical data centres, organisations can rent these resources from a cloud provider and pay only for what they use. This model helps businesses scale quickly when demand grows and reduce costs when demand falls. Cloud providers also take care of hardware maintenance, security updates and backups, so teams can focus on building their applications. Still, moving to the cloud requires careful planning around data privacy, network reliability and vendor lock-in.
