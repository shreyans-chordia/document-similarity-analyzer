With cloud computing, services like storage, servers, software and databases are provided through the internet. Organisations no longer need to purchase and run their own data centres; they can lease resources from a provider and are billed only for their actual usage. Because of this, a business can grow its capacity rapidly during busy periods and cut spending during quiet ones. The provider handles backups, security patches and hardware upkeep, letting developers concentrate on their products. Still, a cloud migration needs planning for privacy of data, reliable networking and the risk of being locked in to one vendor.
